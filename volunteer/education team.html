Saintly's educational volunteering team is in charge of creating all of the educational content and direction of our platform.

WHAT DO VOLUNTEERS DO?

Student's Helping Students

Saintly education volunteers are the key drivers of the pathway and essence of Saintly. They ideate, plan, and develop the content on our page. Without education, Saintly cannot grow. Saintly tutors can help create and edit learning pathways, write scripts for and record videos, and write articles. As a volunteer, you will be putting things into the world that oters are actively using to carry their learning.

Here is the full list of responsibilities

  • Create and edit learning pathways
  • Write scripts for videos
  • Record and edit educational videos
  • Write articles and create demos
  • Create and think of external content ideas to promote and help studying Saintly
  • Create tutor packets and materials

WHAT IS THE STRUCTURE LIKE?

3 Roles, One Goal

Curriculum Developer

Plan and prioritize what lessons need to be covered next, pick who from the team you think would work best with it. Create detailed curriculum guides and materials.

Video Creator

Create engaging scripts for education videos. Show and present curriculum in an engaging manner. Record videos using scripts and interactive demos to help students learn in a personalized manner.

Article Writer

Write topics glossary articles and suggest demos. Create easy to explain pieces to help students learn.

WHO ARE WE LOOKING FOR?

Leaders and Learners

Saintly values leadership and initiative over knowledge. We believe everybody has the ability to grow into dedicated learners. We look for the following characteristics in our tutors:

Understanding

In order to teach, you need to be able to truly understand. We want our volunteers to be so familiar and confident with their skill that they can explain even the most complex concepts as if they are simple. Our content should take difficult concepts and explain them simply.

Communication

Volunteers will be working in a large and collaborative environment. This requires coordination between groups, people, and responsibilities. We expect volunteers to be able to communicate with each other and work together to achieve a common goal.

Initiative

We aren't just looking for content creators, we are looking for dedicated members of the saintly team who can take initiative and grow our program. Tutoring is just one of the many responsibilities associated with working on Saintly, and we hope to get tutors who will actively contribute outside of their repsonsibilities, without being told what to do.

WHY SHOULD I VOLUNTEER WITH SAINTLY?

A Helping Hand in Free Education

The best way to enrich your learning experience and reach proficiency in a subject is to teach. By volunteering with Saintly, you will not just be helping a community of over two thousand students learn, you'll be deepening your own understanding while getting access to unique and complex curriculum. By working with Saintly, you will

  • Help yourself reach new levels of proficiency and understanding
  • Interact with a community of dedicated learners
  • Get real-world experience that could benefit your resume and applications
  • Be Saintly!

WHO CAN APPLY?

Our Requirements

  • At least 15 years old, and no older than 18
  • Competed in at least one math competition
  • Proficiency in math (completed Algebra 2 or Integrated Math 3 and currently enrolled in Precalculus or equivalent)
  • At least one hour per week to dedicate to responsibilities
  • Readily available access to strong internet connection (must be able to hodl zoom meetings with camera and microphone on)

WHAT IS REQUIRED OF VOLUNTEERS?

Saintly Volunteer, Saintly Behavior

Volunteers are expected to follow a Saintly code of conduct to ensure safe, appropriate, and fair behavior. All applicants and registered volunteers are expected to follow the attatched code of conduct. By applying, you are comitting to following conduct:

  • No harassment
  • No inappropriate or unprofessional communication
  • No direct or unsupervised contact with team members or users
  • No requesting or prodviding unescessary personal information
  • All concerns and events must be reported to Saintly executives
  • Maintain appropriate professional boundaries
