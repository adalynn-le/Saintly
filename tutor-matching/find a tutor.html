FAQs

How does tutoring work?

Saintly's tutoring program is built as a live, personalized embodiment of our curriculum. Students pick tutors or tutoring sessions that fit them best (or use our algorithims to find it for them!) and register for the groups. These sessions, individual, group, or seminar, are led by student experts over zoom. Our tutors use Saintly's tailored curriculum in addition to their own teaching expertise and real-competition math expereince. Meetings are done over Zoom and done online to provide access to all regions.

What's the format like?

All meetings are done online with at least one student teacher and volunteer. Students can request sessions to either be one-on-one, in a small group of 3-5, a large class of 5-10, or a "seminar" dedicated to teaching one individual subject in extreme depth. With the consent of all students involved, we record the majority of our meetings for documentation, accountability, and further resources.

How much does it cost?

$0, forever! Saintly is built to ensure that competition doesn't cost a thing!

What will I learn?

Your curriculum will be tutored to you. You can request certain topics and specify weak topics when you request a session. All curriculum is based off of Saintly's curated documentation, articles, and practice, but is expanded for a live and adaptable format.

Who's my tutor?

After entering some basic information, Saintly's algorithim will sort tutors best suited to your needs. You can read through their descriptions and ratings to pick the ones best for you! All tutors are high school student (less than 18) volunteers dedicated to expanding advanced critical mathematical thinking throughout all communities.

Who can be tutored?

All students under the age of 18 with access to reliable internet and zoom are elligible to be tutored. A request to be tutored odes not gaurauntee a tutoring position.

How can I get another question answered?

If you have any other questions, feel free to contact us at saintly.math@gmail.com
